### Strategy 2: The "Processing Time" Ad Hack (Revenue)
Here is the secret weapon of client-side tools: **Dwell Time.** Ad networks pay based on how long a user stays on the page and how many ads they view while there.

Your tool processes the file **in the browser**. This takes time (CPU cycles).

**The Play:** Use the processing time as an ad canvas.
*   When a user drops a 50MB video into **GS-Video** or a 100-page PDF into **GS-PDF**, show a clean, satisfying progress bar: *"Crunching numbers locally to protect your privacy... 45% complete."*
*   **Right next to that progress bar is a high-value, highly visible ad.** 
*   Because the user is literally watching the progress bar, their eyes are glued to the screen. They *cannot* look away. This guarantees high Viewability rates, which tells Google/ad networks your site is premium, driving up your RPM (Revenue Per Mille).
